# Move animations

Generated by `npm run report:anims -- --write`. Presentation only: the battle rules never read any of this.

Moves: 934. Essentials pack animation first: 212. PokeRogue's own: 639. Borrowed (limited): 83.
Families in use: 26 of 26.

Each move tries, in order: the director's Essentials pack animation (`ess-*`, imported by `npm run data:pack-anims`), PokeRogue's animation, then its family effect (`src/ui/battleFx/familyFx.ts`) in its type colour. Reduced or off motion skips all three.

## Known limits

- **Z-Moves and Max/G-Max Moves** borrow a same-type animation: none of the packs (Essentials 21.1 UIE, EBDX, Deluxe Battle Kit, Enhanced Battle UI, Raid Battles) has attack animations for them. They add the signature family burst on top, and their transformation moments have their own cinematics (`src/ui/battleFx/cinematics.ts`).
- **EBDX's 442 scripted move animations** are Ruby code, so they are re-timed by hand (`docs/status/ebdx-scripted-anims-1.md`): 479 moves play one (every EBDX move we have); draft timings can be tuned by hand.
- Essentials' per-sprite colour overlay isn't carried over (darkening tones are).
