Pocket Raiders

A Pokémon roguelike you can replay in your browser.

About

Pocket Raiders is a deterministic, seeded roguelike: pick a trainer and a starter, build a team across regions, beat gym leaders, rivals and the Pokémon League, and carry a legacy into the next run. It runs in a web browser and installs as an offline app.

Facts

  • Play: awsomsause.github.io/pkmn
  • Made with TypeScript, Phaser and Vite
  • Fan project. Pokémon and its characters belong to their owners; the project repository is private.
