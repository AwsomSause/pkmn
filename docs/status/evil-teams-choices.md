# Evil Teams and permanent choices (major update spec §16, §17)

- **Evil Teams** (`core/evil/`, `src/data/evilTeams.json`, run save v32): 10 teams (Rocket, Aqua, Magma, Galactic, Plasma, Flare, Skull, Aether, Yell, Star), each with a style (openly hostile, secret, or a legitimate front), philosophy, motive, goal, four ranks (grunt, specialist, executive, leader) with their own sprite, Pokémon and lines, a battle rule (`swarm` +1 Pokémon, `elite` −1 Pokémon +2 levels, `shadow` first Pokémon is a snaggable Shadow Pokémon), team spoils and a hunted Legendary. One team per run, picked by a seed hash at stage 2.
  - **Storyline**: 7 forced beats, one every 3 stages (suspicious trainers → evidence → they know your face → executive → executive returns, with a defector → the real plan → the hideout). Every choice moves the story; the hideout ends it by battle, a defector's back door, exposing them (kept the evidence + stopped an operation, no battle), or a deal (a permanent Pact relic). Defeated/exposed → "A Legend Stirs" (their Legendary as a wild battle).
  - **Regional operations**: one per region, 11 kinds (facility, steal, research, legendary, trade, recruit, interfere, control, experiment, artificial, habitat). Shut it down (battle), sneak in disguised (Charisma), leave it, or help. Stopping adds a lasting upside, letting it through a lasting downside (run modifiers, e.g. catch rate, prices, healing, Legendary odds); an operation still running when you leave the region succeeds.
  - **Heat** (0–10): +1 level per 3 heat on their battles, +1 Pokémon at 6, ambush stops at 4. Each operation they pull off adds a level.
  - **Recurring villains**: executive and leader remember meetings (met/won/lost); their team grows (+1 Pokémon and +1 level per past meeting) and their line changes (first, after you beat them, after they beat you, grudging respect).
  - **Rescue missions** (from beat 3): a holding pen of stolen Pokémon; break them out (battle) or sneak them out (Speed) and one joins you, or walk away (remembered).
  - **Internal conflict** (from beat 4): the executive and a specialist feud; side with the executive (−heat, money, Evil), turn the specialist into an inside source (a defector, opens the hideout back door), or set them against each other (Charisma: their operation here collapses).
  - Secret/front teams also run a disguised merchant booth (buy from them, or unmask them).
- **Permanent choices** (`core/evil/choices.ts`): the run's choice history (`story.choices`, first answer stands, weight 1–3). New event effect `choice` and condition `choice`; Evil Team operations, endings, feuds and recruits record themselves. 8 new decision events with 11 follow-ups (group "Lasting Choices"): help/ignore/rob a stranded trainer (ally battle or revenge), Rangers vs Prospectors (lasting relic + the other side becomes an enemy), rescue a Riolu (it joins later), shrine offering (relic + Legendary beast), a fork in the road (mountain/coast/forest, run-long modifiers and weather), help your rival (gift or a grudge rematch), trust a stranger's map (Legendary lair or scam), fund a researcher (bigger payoff later).
- **UI**: the Story screen lists the team (style, goal, rule, heat, operations, villain records) and the choice history (◆ by weight).
- Tests: `tests/unit/evilTeams.test.ts` (15), `tests/e2e/evilTeams.spec.ts`.
- **Plaza** (`core/evil/plaza.ts`, spec §33): a disguised member walks in the Plaza crowd (60% of stages once the story starts; secret/front teams wear their front's name); People offers Unmask (a one-round Battle Club fight with their grunt team and rule; a win gives +heat, a reward, their stash and a recorded choice; a loss is friendly) or Buy from them (spoils, −heat, Evil). The executive you met returns (40%) with a line from your history, or supplies if you made a deal. Stalls cost 15% more while a trade/control operation is running or went through in the region.
- **Limited**: no experimental moves or team-only gear (spoils are existing items); team members don't defect mid-battle; the shadow rule reuses the Snag system; operation art reuses the team sprites; the original Shadow World factions (Umbra Corps, Gilded Talon) stay separate.
