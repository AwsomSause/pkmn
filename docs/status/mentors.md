- Mentors and apprentices (spec §15): 12 mentors (`src/data/mentors.json`, `core/mentors/index.ts`), one per category (battle, breeder, researcher, explorer, coordinator, former champion, rival, gym leader, Elite Four, legend scholar, item, evolution), each with background, personality, strength, weakness, 4 lessons (3 unlock with trust, 1 "advanced" after graduating), a mission and a graduation. Choose one on the Run Prep desk (last row, "Mentor"); 4 are open at the start, the rest unlock through runs, wins or trust with another mentor (Hub, Events, **Mentors**). Trust (+6 per badge, events) and loyalty (events) grow; lessons apply once through `perkEffects`; a fellow apprentice (potential, personality, strength, weakness, types, learning speed, interest) grows per badge and rivals you; 18 "Mentors" events (12 per mentor, 6 shared: training, disagreement, fellow duel, mission offer, personal story, final lesson) with one always offered per stage. Trust 60+ at the end is a graduation (devoted or independent by loyalty); the bond, lessons and the fellow apprentice (who can return as a veteran) persist. Saves: profile 15 (`mentorBonds`), run 30 (`mentor`, `prep.mentorId`). Limited: apprentice rivalry is a standing, not a fight; the run's mentor status shows in the Run Prep summary, events and the end-of-run lines (no map HUD).
