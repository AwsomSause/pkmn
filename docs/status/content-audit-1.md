- Content audit 1 (abilities and forms): Eelevate (Mega Eelektross) now raises its highest stat after a KO and counts as floating for Arena Trap, the AI and battle shadows. Levitate and Eelevate now block Earthquake, Surf-style "hits underground" Ground moves were slipping through; Gravity and Smack Down still ground them, Thousand Arrows still hits. A Mega or Primal always uses its own ability (a hidden-ability base no longer keeps its old one). Battle forms reached by an ability (Zen Mode, Stance Change, Schooling, Forecast...) or a held item now count as working in the content report instead of "disabled". New `tests/unit/contentAudit.test.ts` runs every Pokémon with each of its abilities and every supported move through a short battle sim and checks each supported ability is named in battle code. Still disabled on purpose: Tatsugiri Droopy/Stretchy Megas, Original Magearna Mega and five Delta battle forms (no way to reach them), 17 signature Z-Moves.
