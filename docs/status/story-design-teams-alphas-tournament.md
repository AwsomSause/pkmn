# Story design content 1: original Evil Teams, Alphas, Exhibition Bracket

Status: supported (Story Run only), with the limits below.

- **Six original Evil Teams** (Design v2 Part IV) are now real teams in `src/data/evilTeams.json` (`storyOnly: true`): The Mythic Marauders (Ashgrove, Quill, Voss; after Equinar), The Hollow Choir (Ravenscar, Brother Cull, Sister Pennant; Hollow Shepherd), The Ironvein Combine (Strand, Pym, Ealing; Veinheart), The Pale Cartographers (Rook, Quay, Tace; Hollow Shepherd and Brinecrown), The Wildfire Accord (Greaves, Marden, Okafor; Verdelis), The Gilt Circle (Ashdown, Ferrante, Dunne; Lumeria). Each has 4 ranks with lines, a doctrine team, battle rule, spoils, 4 operations, and gets the full 7-beat storyline from the shared templates.
- **Story Runs draw only these six; Classic never draws them** (`plannedEvilTeamId`), so Classic's team pick is the same roll over the same list as before.
- **Ten Alphas** (Part IX-B) as Story-only map events (`src/data/events/storyAlphas.json`): Quarry King, Weeping Lantern, Frostbound, Static Choir, Hushed One, Black Tide, Orchard Warden, Unwelcome Guest, Sleeper, Rooftop Watcher. Each has the Boss Kit cue in its intro and the five outcomes as run flags (`alpha-<id>-subdued/befriended/relocated/driven-back/left`): fight (a strong wild battle, +6 levels), approach calmly (attribute check; it joins), lead it away (chance), or leave it.
- **Exhibition Bracket** (Part IX tournaments; `src/data/events/storyTournament.json`): Registration stop (enter, spectate, skip) then three rooms: round of eight, a semifinal with the power-failure interruption (fight on in the dark with a hidden foe, or evacuate the crowd), and a final against your rival. Win: trophy money, Star Piece, Chronicle moment.
- New event condition `storyRun`; tournament and Alpha fights use `pseudo` battles, so a loss sends you back to the map and never ends the run.
- Tests: `tests/unit/storyDesignContent.test.ts`.
- Limits: villains use existing pack sprites as stand-ins (no original art). Alphas are boosted wild fights, not the raid/totem wrapper (no shields, phases or ally calls yet), and their arcs/aftermath scenes are flags only. Only the Exhibition Bracket format is built (Group and Knockout, Masked, Team Doubles are not); bracket opponents are generic, not seeded from `circuitRanking()`. Lieutenants beyond the 4 ranks (e.g. Cass Verrin, Ilse Rennick for the Marauders) appear in operation text only.
