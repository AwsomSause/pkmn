# Item and rival loose ends (plans audit 2026-11-07)

- **Species Z-Crystals (17):** Aloraichium, Decidium, Eevium, Incinium, Kommonium, Lunalium, Lycanium, Marshadium, Mewnium, Mimikium, Pikanium, Pikashunium, Primarium, Snorlium, Solganium, Tapunium, Ultranecrozium Z. New item effect `speciesZCrystal` (species list, base move, Z-Move). With the Z-Ring and the crystal, that species turns that one move into its own Z-Move (never misses, once per battle, replaces the type Z-Move for that move; other moves still use the type crystal). The 17 signature Z-Moves in `moves.json` are now supported (`zmove` flag, so they can't be learned). Offered as rare rewards once you have the Z-Ring and a party member knows the move. `halveTarget` can now be a fraction (Guardian of Alola takes 75%). Art: item library. Limits: Extreme Evoboost is +2 to all five stats; Stoked Sparksurfer always paralyses; Light That Burns the Sky/Searing Sunraze/Menacing Moonraze ignore the target's ability; Genesis Supernova sets no terrain; Splintered Stormshards removes no terrain; Catastropika, Pulverizing Pancake etc. are plain high-power hits. Necrozma forms: only `necrozma-dusk` (Solganium) and `necrozma-ultra` exist; Dawn Wings has no species yet.
- **Bottle Caps: still not built.** The game has no IVs (Kice's IV/EV decision is open), so a Bottle Cap would be a placeholder. **Fan/Fallen Mega Stones:** every Mega form that exists in the game already has its stone (145 stones, 146 forms); the Drive "Fallen" stones have no Mega form to point at.
- **Rival win rate:** `npm run sim:overhaul -- 100` on current main: early rival fights 177 won of 299 = **59.2%** (band 40-60%), so no tuning. Mid and late can't be measured: the smart bot loses every run (26 to wild, 74 to trainers) before the mid-game. No number was changed.
- **Rarity caps** (`rarity.json`): regional forms sit `offRegionShift` (1) tier rarer in wild pools outside their home region (`rarityIn`, `gatePool(..., regionId)`); Trade Post offers are capped at Epic (no Legendary/Mythical trades); shops sell items up to Rare (the Black Market ignores the cap); reward drafts offer no Epic items before 2 badges. Tests in `rarity.test.ts`.
- **Legendary roles in battle:** a Legendary/Mythical enters the field with one stage for its role (striker +Spe, powerhouse +best attack, wall +Def, support +SpD, controller +Accuracy), on either side, once per stay (`legendaryRoles.json` `entry`, `roleEntryBoost`). Silent (no log line), like X items. Bosses with a role get it too.
- **Power check counts Tera and Z:** `powerScore().withAccess` = rating x (1 + Tera% + Z%), with a species Z-Crystal counting more (`balance.powerBudget.access`: 4% / 4% / 10%, headroom 1.12). The `over-power-budget` gate fails a species over its cap x headroom. Nothing is over today (worst: Lairon-Delta at 1.07 of cap).
- **Grow ideas:** (1) 40 hand-written rest-stop scenes for Delta Pokémon and (2) 51 for species the pack has no lore for, in `src/data/momentsExtra.json`, read by party narration. (3) Quirk lines for camp by hour and festival day (new season, milestone, real date), 12 quirk categories x 4 moments, `src/data/quirkTimes.json` + `core/overhaul/quirkTimes.ts`; shown on half of camp stops. Text only; no save change.
