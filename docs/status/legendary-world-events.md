- Legendary world events (spec §19; no save version bump): a Legendary gate (✦, "Legendary" button) can appear on a stage map (`core/legendary/legendary.ts`, `placeLegendaryGate`, 12% of stages, 1 per region, rarer events thinner). It opens a temporary side stage built on the expedition framework: the briefing shows the omen, lore, the stage rules and says the chance is gone when the stage ends; the player is never forced in. **Data:** `legendaryEvents.json` (generated by `npm run data:legendary-events` from `tools/build-legendary-events.ts` and `tools/legendary/bespoke.ts`) holds 89 events (every Legendary/Mythical base form except cosmog, cosmoem, kubfu, phione, meltan) and their finale Totems (`legendary: true`, never drawn for dens or ordinary expeditions); content loading merges them into `expeditions` and `totems`. 12 are hand-designed (Kyogre flood, Groudon scorched earth, Rayquaza tempest, Giratina distortion, Dialga, Palkia, Lugia, Ho-Oh, Mewtwo, Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres); the rest use a type-themed template. **Stage rules:** weather/terrain per fight by layer (Giratina rolls from a pool per fight, seeded), type affinity (HP recovery) and peril (HP chip, never below 1) after each stop, paths that collapse (`closes`) and routes that rearrange (`distort`), an event-only wild table, event-only NPCs at mystery stops, a 2-3 phase finale Totem, special rewards (type Tera Shard, Full Restore, a chance to keep the Legendary). **Triggers:** region range, time of day (`timeOfDay`), stage theme, existing event conditions (`requires`/`any`: party type, tags, friendship...), party types make it likelier (`favoured`). **Saves:** `run.legendary` (offered, completed, failed, extracted, gates per region, outcomes; optional field) and `blocked` on expedition route nodes (optional). Tests `tests/unit/legendaryEvents.test.ts`. Limited: template events share their type's rules; per-event music and art reuse the expedition track and a colour wash (`visual.tint`), no custom maps or unique animations yet; "loops" are modelled as rearranged routes; no Raid-based Legendary events yet; the briefing is text only.
