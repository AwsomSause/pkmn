- Hub Town, part 1 (Hub Town spec 2026-11-01, phases 2 and 3): the Hub is now a walkable Pokémon town (112×84 tiles) built from the AwsomSause/Pokemon-Memories tilesets by `tools/hubtown/build.py` (layout in `tools/hubtown/layout.py`). You walk as your most-used Pokémon (or one you pick under Walking Pokémon; party and runs never change); arrows walk, Shift runs, Z enters a door or talks, Esc opens the menu (settings, Walking Pokémon, save data, tools, back to title). Every old Hub room opens from a building: Competitive Arena, Hall of Memories, Achievement Hall, Mentor Dojo, Research Institute, Player HQ, Trainer Boutique, Pokémon Center, Pokémon Bank, Event Hall, Run Prep Center (spawn faces it; Enter starts a run), Network Post, Corner Nook, Starter Sanctuary, Berry Orchard, the Event Board, the Pocket Town gate and the Departure Gate (both ask first). Legacy Display: the current run and up to five past Trainers stand on six plaza pedestals with their Aces; face one and press Z for the record. Strange Merchant: 4 odd items a day from `src/data/hubMerchant.json`, one of each, paid in Bank Credits, delivered as Pocket Town's next-run boon. The growing-Hub props (lanterns, planters, banners, trophy statues, fountain spray, star sparkles) now appear around the plaza. Saves: profile v17 (`hubTown`: walking Pokémon, merchant day, bought list). Tests `tests/unit/hubTown.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/hubTown.spec.ts`; Hub specs updated. Not done yet (next PRs): wandering trainers and Pokémon that change over time, ambient water and sound, history-aware NPC dialogue, the soft plaza edge polish.
- Hub Town, part 2 (spec §8, §9 and Kice's 2026-11-02 asks): a crowd of 10 to 20 trainers and 12 to 26 Pokémon wanders the town (`core/hubTown/roster.ts`, `ui/hubTown/crowd.ts`). Who is out changes every half hour of local time, the crowd grows as you finish runs and meet Pokémon, and the Pokémon are ones you have met. Face one and press Z: trainers talk, with lines built from your real history such as past Trainers, their Aces, Champions, the Pokémon that beat you and your best badge count (`core/hubTown/chatter.ts`; records are only read). Pokémon hop and react. The plaza now fades into its sand ring with a DS-style pixel dither, and the ring is an organic shape instead of a box (`tools/hubtown/build.py`). Water glints and drifting petals play with full motion only. Crowd and chatter come from a hash of the profile and the time window, never from gameplay RNG. Tests are in `tests/unit/hubTown.test.ts` (crowd, chatter) and `tests/e2e/hubTown.spec.ts`.
