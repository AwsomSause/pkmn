- W11 follow-up (settings v13): real interface skins, not only colours (`core/presentation/skins.ts` data, `ui/skinPaint.ts` drawing). Classic Plum keeps the pixel windows; Frutiger Aero turns every window into glossy glass; DS Touch uses flat rounded cards with a hard drop shadow, a square cursor and snappy motion (no pop-overshoot); Wii Channel uses glossy pill tiles with soft layered shadows, a pale wash backdrop and bouncy elastic motion; Cyber-Maximalism uses chamfered neon plates with scanlines, a neon cursor, a perspective-grid backdrop and fast glitchy motion. Panels switch through `paintWindow` (widgets) and `paintGlass` (aero), the cursor through `WiiCursor`, backdrops through `aeroBackdrop`, motion through `uiTween`/`springTuning` (`feel`). Every gamepad action (up, down, left, right, confirm, back) is remappable: `gamepadMap` in settings, one row per action in Settings > Controls, and "Remap by pressing buttons" listens for the next press (`ui/gamepad.ts` `listenForButton`); a button already in use swaps. Every jingle and fanfare gets a lyric-style caption (all `me.*` jingles, the four victory fanfares, the four raid capture jingles; `core/presentation/uiPrefs.ts` `jingleCaption`, shown from `ui/audio.ts`, also when muted). Migration v12 to v13 carries the old confirm/back buttons over. Tests in `tests/unit/w11Ui.test.ts`.
- Still limited: the Settings screens use the UIE Options background art in every skin (only panels, cursor and motion change there); skins redraw on the next screen you open; panels are drawn shapes, not new art files.
