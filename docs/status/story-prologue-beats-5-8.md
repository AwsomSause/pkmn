# Story prologue Beats 5-8 (quiz, reveal, backstory, first partner)

- `PrologueScene` now plays Beat 5 (the quiz: 12 questions in the full prologue, the 6 quick ones in Quick Start, answers shown in a fixed per-question order that is not the data order, Back goes to the previous question), Beat 6 (the reveal: Match, Complement, Surprise with type and Temperament, Retake until Beat 8), Beat 7 (all enabled backstories with the design doc's pitch line) and Beat 8 (pick one of the three). Skip Prologue goes straight to Beats 7-8 with a neutral draft.
- Short Intake: "Begin the Story Run" asks for a backstory, then a partner from the last quiz's three plus every line unlocked in the Archive, or the 6-question **Intake Interview**; then Trainer Select in Story Run mode.
- Rules in `core/story2/intake.ts` (`quizQuestions`, `quizPool`, `revealPicks` (quick answers weigh double; seed = answers + unlocked set), `intakeChoices`, `nextRunStart`). The choice is saved in `profile.story.nextRun` (new defaulted field, no save bump) and the quiz result in `profile.story.quiz.lastResult`.
- `GameSession.newRun` on a story campaign: the chosen line's first stage becomes the only Starter (`starterOverride`), the backstory goes into `v2.protagonist.backstory` (`startBackstory` already honours it), then `nextRun` is cleared. Classic runs ignore it. So Story Runs now start with real Remembrance lines (Heartbond, Feats, Starter arcs, signature moves all apply).
- Tests: `tests/unit/storyIntake.test.ts` (6), `tests/e2e/prologue.spec.ts` (Quick Start through Beats 5-8; Short Intake backstory and partner).
- Limits: the straight-lining joke, Vance's per-answer reaction lines, the Professor's Pick +10 Heartbond and Pokémon pictures on the reveal are not built; Beat 9 (Field Trial) is still skipped.
