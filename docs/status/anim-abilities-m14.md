- Ability animation round 2 (M14 gaps, 6.1, 6.2, 6.7): ability banners now use the reference video's look (screen dims, letterbox bars, caption top-left, the Pokémon punches in, red anger star for Intimidate) and play a pack animation from `public/assets/battle-anims` (`abilityPackAnim`: per ability, else per category; Intimidate's `scary-face` lands on the foe). Signature effects for Levitate, Speed Boost/Quick Feet/Swift Swim, Download/Trace, Pressure, Moxie/Beast Boost, Intimidate (foe flinches). Abilities the engine doesn't announce now animate too: `impliedAbility` (core/presentation/abilityAnims.ts) infers weather/terrain setters, absorb immunities, end-of-turn healers, post-KO boosts and form changes from the event plus the ability on that side (`planCues` 6th argument `abilityOf`); the engine and its events are unchanged. Registry fields added: `trigger`, `sound`, `text`, `fallback`; ability groups (passive/offensive/defensive/healing/stat/weather/environmental). Battle text: stat changes (ATTACK ▲▲) and status (BURNED!) get animated words. No save change. Limited: abilities that change damage silently (Adaptability, Technician …) and contact punishers still don't animate; the unannounced-ability inference can miss a case where two abilities fit one event.
