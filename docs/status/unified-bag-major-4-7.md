- Major Update §4 + §7, unified Bag (`core/bag.ts`, `ui/fieldMenus.ts` `openBagMenu`): the map's Items menu is gone; the Bag shows everything the run owns in pocket tabs (All, Medicine, Poké Balls, Held, Battle, Evolution, Key & passive, Other, Map; empty pockets hide; Left/Right or a click switches). Each row: icon, name, rarity (Bag items take the rarity of the reward card that gives them; shop-only balls have none), count, and who holds it or what it does; the footer has the full description, holders, stack limit and whether it is passive. Held items are listed with their holders and never counted twice (they are not Bag items). Picking a held item takes it off a holder into the Bag (`run.looseHeld`, optional field, no save bump); picking a loose one gives one copy to a party member (slot, stack and species rules apply; outside battle only; `session.takeOffHeld` / `giveHeld`). The Map pocket keeps the old Items menu's actions (bikes, Escape Rope, VS Seeker) and intel (Fame Checker, gym preview, Field License). The battle Bag is unchanged.
