# Story design content 2: event templates, Eevee Crossroads, party chemistry and camp scenes

Status: supported (Story Run only), with the limits below.

- **30 event templates** (Design v2 Part XI: The Mediator, The Champion Notices, The Quiet Hand ... The Team-Up) as story scenes `ev_tpl_<id>`, each with 2 beats and 2 or 3 choices. Source: `tools/story/build-world-scenes.mjs` writes compact `src/data/story/worldScenes.json` (expanded by `worldScenes.ts`) and `text/worldScenes.en.json`; rebuild then `npm run data:story`.
- **Eevee Crossroads** (Part II-F): the eight written offers. At a Recovery Stop, an Eevee of level 20+ (`balance.story.world.eeveeLevel`) is offered a form whose type a party member or the stage has (Espeon in odd Acts, Umbreon in even ones, Sylveon at Heartbond 3+); at most 3 offers a run. "Let it change" evolves it (`evolve_eevee` effect), "Stay as Eevee" sets a flag.
- **Party chemistry** (Part II): the 12 chemistry types (Rivalry, Mentor and Student, Protective, Dislike, Inseparable, Jealousy, Comfort, Daredevil Duo, Odd Couple, Caretaker, Idol and Fan, Old Friends) worked out per pair from the 16 Temperaments, Party Life pair stages, evolution stage and family (`core/story2/worldScenes.ts` `chemistryType`, `partyChemistry`, at most 3 shown). **24 camp scenes** (2 per type) play at Recovery Stops for the first pair with a type, naming both Pokémon.
- **When they play** (`worldPick`, asked last in `module.ts`): only at Stops the backstory, rival scenes, the Starter arc and quest arcs leave free; never move the backstory's gap; templates at most 8 a run and 2 an Act, in a stable per-run order (string hash, no RNG), each only from its Act and when its needs hold (another template first, Heartbond level, the Hisui backstory, the Gilt Circle as the team). Template choices add `world_*` counters and flags, so backstory ending grids never move.
- New tokens `{a}`, `{b}`, `{place}`; new kickers "At Camp" and "Crossroads". Balance: `balance.story.world`.
- Tests: `tests/unit/storyWorldScenes.test.ts`; `storyPlay.ts` strategies skip world scenes like Starter arc scenes.
- Limits: chemistry types have no battle effects yet (story use only) and no party-menu icons; templates are scenes with choices, not the battles/arcs their outcomes describe (e.g. The Mediator's exhibition battle, The Follower joining are flags); roles are written generically ("your rival", "a Gym Leader") instead of filled from the cast; free Stops are few, so a run sees about 1 to 3 templates.
