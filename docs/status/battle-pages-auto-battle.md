# Battle pages and Auto-Battle (spec §23, §32)

Saves: run 28 (optional `battle.autoUsed`, no-op migration), settings 10 (`autoBattle`, `autoExplain`). Balance: `difficulty.autoBattleXp` = 0.5.

- **Pages (§23)**: an Attack · Party · Item · Auto · ⓘ tab strip (`ui/battleTabs.ts`) sits on the bar's top edge (main menus) or in the title band (Bag and Party lists). The active page lights up and bounces; click/tap a tab, or press Q / E to move between pages, I for details, T for Auto. The root menu keeps Fight/Bag/Pokémon (+ Tera/Z/Max, Cheer, Brace, Rally; three columns when there are more than four, so nothing runs off the bar). The I key opens a panel over the bar: on the Attack page the focused move (type, category, power, accuracy, PP, priority, effect text from `core/battle/moveText.ts`, flags, effectiveness); elsewhere the battlefield (weather, terrain, screens, hazards, Momentum, raid cheers, rival notes), your Pokémon (nature, mood, stance, status, stat changes, held items) and the foe (`core/battle/conditions.ts`). Party page: picking a Pokémon opens Send out / Summary / Stance (stats, moves, held items, how it fights).
- **Auto-Battle (§32)** (`core/battle/autoBattle.ts`): pure, RNG-free. Each Pokémon's profile comes from its nature, stat spread, bond trait, emotion, confidence, mentor path or taught stance (Trainer Instillment, saved in `bond.habits.stance`; a willful Pokémon that distrusts you half listens), fear (hurt or outmatched) and anger (fallen teammates). Moves are scored with the trainer-AI `moveValue` (damage, accuracy, type, KO, status, set-up) then bent by the profile; it also heals, guards, switches out of hopeless or dangerous matchups, picks who to send out, and uses Tera, Z-Move, Dynamax, Rally, Brace and raid cheers. A hash of uid + turn + move gives small differences between same-species Pokémon (no RNG stream). The pick shows for a moment (Esc takes control back), with the reason when "Auto-Battle explanations" is on.
- **EXP**: the first Auto-Battle action marks the battle (`autoUsed`); the whole battle's EXP is then halved, even if you go back to manual. The cost is told before it turns on (confirm card) and on the first pick of each battle.
- Party and Bag lists are docked in the bottom bar (Picker `dock` option), so the arena and HP boxes stay visible. Tests: `tests/unit/autoBattle.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/battlePages.spec.ts`.

- **Auto uses Bag items** (follow-up): a hurt Pokémon (earlier when cautious, afraid or caring, later when bold) uses the smallest potion that covers what is missing, or the biggest it has; a Pokémon with sleep/freeze (or a cautious one with any status) uses a cure. It never heals when it can finish the foe or a healing move is already its pick.
- **Auto-Battle in doubles** (follow-up): each Pokémon is judged on its own (same profile and scoring as singles) against each foe, and aims at the foe it likes best; one Tera/Z-Move/Dynamax per kind and one item per turn across the pair; no switching out, cheers, Brace or Rally in doubles (the menus don't offer them either). After a faint it picks the best replacement not already on the field. Test in `tests/unit/autoBattle.test.ts`.
