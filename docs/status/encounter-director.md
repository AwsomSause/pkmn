- Encounter Director + Combat Threat Rating (left-out plan 1.2, Balancing Phases 4–10): `core/encounterDirector.ts` is the one path for generated Pokémon: gated pool → weighted draw → evolve → validate (known species, a supported move, the level's BST ceiling, the source's threat budget) → up to `balance.threat.retries` retries → the weakest valid draw as a logged fallback. Used by wild fights, trainer teams, fishing, event battles and event `join` rewards (which now stay under the region cap / party cap). Authored Pokémon (boss teams, an event's own species list, a Bell's legendary, rival ghost teams, raid bosses) are not re-rolled but audited and logged as named exceptions. `core/threat.ts` rates a built Pokémon (100 = all-70 stats with a 60-power same-type attack; stats at level, best attack, speed, setup/recovery/status/priority, strong abilities); budgets per tier (easy/standard/challenging/elite) rise with level (`balance.threat.budgets`, `sourceTiers`). Report: `docs/quality/encounter-director-report.md` + `.json` (10,000 wild and 2,000 trainer teams per early stage: levels, species, rarity, evolution stage, threat, rejections, fallbacks, invalid, locked picks, one-hit KOs), written by `tests/unit/encounterDirector.test.ts`. Limited: party loss and win rates come from the playtest bot, not this report; quests have no Pokémon generator of their own (they use events); trades, eggs, fossils and reunions keep their authored species; the threat numbers are hand-tuned starting values. +100/region deferred to part G. Map-item pools (Lures, Flutes' type pools) are still not availability-gated.
