- Rival depth (Kice major-update spec §9, §10, §11, §21, §29; `core/rivals/major.ts`, `monRivals.ts`, `sacrifice.ts`, `battle/sacrificeRule.ts`, `data/majorRivals.json`; no save bump, every new field is optional). **§21 guaranteed major rival:** the stage-2 first rival now always becomes a full rival record (`rival.major`), win or lose. It is a semi-canon character from the class's names (Blue, Gary, Silver, Barry, Hop): the starter it picked is its ace, and its other slots come from its canonical team (same slot, same family every meeting, evolved for the level). It returns every 2 stages, ahead of other rivals, and is never driven to retire. **§9 ace:** after each meeting `rival.aceLog` keeps the ace's form, level, moves, held item (a type booster from the 2nd meeting), wins/losses and changes; the Story screen lists it. **§10:** the move of yours that knocked out the most of its team is studied (max 2) and the ace uses it as a signature move (awakened, +20%); a move it can't learn becomes its closest same-type move. The Pokémon that knocked out its ace gets a trained counter (a learnable super-effective move, max 2) that the ace uses whenever that Pokémon is in front of it. Both persist and are told in the intro in the character's voice. **§11 Pokémon rivals:** a wild Pokémon that knocks out one of yours (or fights one in a long duel down to 30% HP) may (seeded 35%, one at a time) become that Pokémon's rival, with a mark ("a torn ear"). It takes the first wild battle of every later stage (planned level +2, evolved), gets "RIVAL!" and +1 Atk, can't be caught, and keeps a history. A hostile trainer rival (the major one first) may catch it off-screen after 2 meetings (seeded 40% per stage); from then on it is always last on that rival's team. **§29 sacrifice:** in a trainer battle where that rival carries it, if it is the last Pokémon in reserve and the attacker's hit would knock out its rival, it jumps in front (armed per battle by a seeded 50% roll): it is gone for the run, your Pokémon is fully healed, cured and enraged (+2 priority on its next 2 moves), with a red burst and its own line. The run records it (chronicle, "Saved by a Rival" title) and the saved Pokémon reacts when it meets that species again. Tests `tests/unit/rivalDepth.test.ts`. Limited: singles only (no doubles); a stage with no wild battle skips the Pokémon rival; no Camp scene or unique sacrifice animation beyond the burst; no new custom move data (studied moves reuse real moves).
