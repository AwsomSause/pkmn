- Overdrive behaviors (spec 8): three new moments decided by the battle engine on the "hyper" stream (`core/battle/engine.ts`: `hyperDefiance`, `hyperIntercept`, `hyperAwaken`; data in `story.json`, effects `defy`/`intercept`/`awaken`). **Defiance**: after 2 hits taken (`battle.hyper.taken`), a fatal hit leaves the Pokémon at 1 HP, strikes the foe for 25% of its max HP (foe never below 1) and costs Speed and Defense. **Protective Instinct**: the most bonded benched teammate takes 60% (at least what keeps the front Pokémon up) of a fatal hit; the teammate is never knocked out by it, and is skipped if it cannot bear the hit. **Emotional Awakening**: a Pokémon at 35% HP or less gains a temporary ability for its time in front by current emotion (Determination Guts, Bravery Adaptability, Justice Filter, Kindness Regenerator, Patience Unaware, Integrity Thick Fat, Perseverance Multiscale; Guts without an emotion) and pays one stat stage. All three: friendship gates, chance from attributes, once per battle per Pokémon, one moment per turn, hyper animations and memories like the other moments. Save: run save unchanged (`battle.hyper.taken` has a default). Limited: not in double battles (they use the old knockout path); Defiance/Protective Instinct need a single opposing Pokémon in front. Still open from spec 8: unique temporary moves, altered move properties, environmental/new battle conditions.
- Pushed Beyond Its Limits (spec 8): a damaging move from a Pokémon at 50% HP or less cannot miss and hits 40% harder after costing 15% max HP (never below 1); once per battle per Pokémon (`tryLimitBreak` in `core/battle/engine.ts`, effect `limitBreak`). Not in double battles.
