# W1 batch 2: skill moments, arena, deals (plain language)

What the player can do now:
- Turn on **Skill prompts** in Settings: drag to aim Thunder-type moves, tap to shout before a move, ring-throw a Poké Ball at a weakened wild foe, pick the crit camera angle, double-tap to confirm Mega/Z/Tera, and beat the clock in countdown events.
- Offer/accept a **deal** with a beaten-down trainer, **scan** a foe for weaknesses, **smash** the arena (rocks/lava/flood) and watch hazards hit both sides every 3 turns.
- Psychic allies **mind-read** a foe move; fainted allies leave a **grudge** (rage buff); spectators may toss a buff; matching-type doubles attacks fire a **combo**.
- Pin 4 items to a **hotbar** (P in the Bag), see a best-target marker, adaptive music, skippable boss cinematics.
- Type lures bias the next encounter; map nodes show a likely-species list; Battle Club lets you watch and wager on a 3v3 exhibition (once per stage).

Limits: arena, deal and skill prompts are singles only. The exhibition is a power-score simulation. No save version bump (new fields have defaults).
