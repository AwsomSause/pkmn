- Run Journal, Hall of Memories and the persistent world (APPROVED.md §3, §7 Battle Replay, §8; `core/memories/`, `data/memories.json`, `scenes/MemoriesScene.ts`, Hub > Events > Memories). No save version bump: every new field is optional or has a default (`profile.memories`, `legacyRun.replays/world`, `run.past`, `story.replays/world`, `battle.memory`, egg/Pokémon `lineage`).
  - **Run Journal / Hall of Memories / Memory Lane / Archive of Fallen Trainers** read the frozen legacy records (`journal.ts`): a written entry per run, up to 5 memorable runs retold as vignettes, every key moment, chronicle line and battle recap, and each lost run's party, victories, final opponent and summary.
  - **Battle Replay** (`replay.ts`): gym, Elite Four, champion, rival, nemesis, ghost, gauntlet and echo fights keep a one-line-per-turn record (`battle.drama.replay`), sealed into `story.replays` (10 a run) and copied onto the legacy record. Written from the same events the drama planner reads, so the battle is never touched. Limited: text only, no reconstructed visuals.
  - **Ghost Trainers, Previous-Run Gauntlet, Echoes** (`encounters.ts`): one memory takes the first ordinary trainer slot of a stage (gauntlet, then a due echo, then a rare ghost, a hash of seed and stage). A ghost is a lost run's team under its Trainer's name, told with where and to what it fell; beating it pays ₽40/level and its memento and lays it to rest in the profile. The Archive Gauntlet event (`pastGauntlet` effect, needs 2 kept teams) sends 3 past teams, one a stage, for ₽2500 and 2 Full Restores. Limited: the ghost uses the ordinary trainer class art, not the old Trainer's sprite; the gauntlet is an event, not a separate mode.
  - **Legacy Pokémon, Descendants, Inheritance Choices** (`heirloom.ts`): a finished run's Ace (Lv 20+) leaves an heirloom with up to four options (Legacy Egg, Egg that knows its signature move, Egg with its nature, or its held keepsake). Pick in Memories > Legacy Heirlooms; the next run starts with it (`GameSession.newRun`, spent once). The descendant keeps a `lineage` and hatches with only that one trait.
  - **Lost-and-Found** (`keepsakes.ts`): items held by past teams come back with their history, as a discounted lot at the Auction House (half of auction stops) and at the Lost-and-Found Desk event. Each is recovered once (the profile records it when the run ends).
  - **World memory** (`world.ts`): skipped side stops and harder roads come back as an Echo trainer 2 stages later (+2 levels, pays ₽30/level and 2 Super Potions); a clear kindness or cruelty (Good/Evil ±8) pays thanks (+₽400) or a toll (−₽200) 2 stages later; a villain operation (3 data-driven ones) runs across 3 stages and is foiled by 2 event clues (+₽800) or succeeds (lose up to 15% of money, max ₽600); a recurring NPC (2 in data) has one beat in each of the first 3 regions, warmer when you helped more than you turned away; an old rival or beaten nemesis from an earlier run turns up once a region (+₽300, +10 friendship).
