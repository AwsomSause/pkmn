- Eggs and Berries (spec §26, §27): **Eggs** have four tiers (Ordinary, Unusual, Rare, Exceptional; `data/eggs.json`) and kinds (standard, regional, event, special, mystery, legacy). Tier is rolled from a hash of the run seed (no existing stream moves) for the origins listed in `eggs.json` (Mystery/Regional/Legacy/Form Egg, Adopted Baby, Event, Rare Nest, Hidden Nest); other origins stay Ordinary. Rarer tiers take longer, give more egg moves, a better shiny chance, bonus hatch levels, higher friendship, a hidden ability, and a Blissful chance. Mystery/Hidden eggs hide their tier until half hatched. Flame Body/Magma Armor/Steam Engine Pokémon warm eggs (2 warmth = 1 extra step). Menus show a progress bar; the hatch screen tints the egg and shows a tier line. Events can pick `origin`/`tier` on the egg effect. **Berries**: Berry Garden stops are now reusable and let a party Pokémon plant a held berry (3 plots, ripe in 1–2 stages, one faster with a Water/Grass type); plots grow each stage (`run.garden`), mutate by seed (Hyper variant or +1 berry) and cross-pollinate (rare berry chance) on harvest. New themed game event **Great Berry Festival** (May 8–22) with 8 festival stops (gates, contest, thief, guardian tree, research tent, merchant, shortage, hidden grove), exclusive Pokémon (Tropius, Shuckle, Applin, Greedent with hidden abilities, Blissful), a seedling effect, objectives and a Berry Baron title. Save: `garden` and the new Egg fields default, so no version bump. Follow-up: soil quality (rich/fair/poor from the stage theme; rich +1 berry, poor halves mutation) and Camp warming (a night at camp moves every Egg one step, never hatching it). Limited: no new berry item entries (the Bag thread has finished; ask Kice which to add).
Four new berries (Roseli, Hyper Roseli, Kee, Maranga; Kee/Maranga raise Defense/Sp. Def when hit, via `berryRetaliate` `stat`) have catalog entries, icons recoloured from the Chilan icon, and can be planted or cross-pollinated.
Egg traders (spec §26 Trading): the Plaza's Trading Post has an Egg traders area with three NPCs (Nest Keeper Orla, Ranger Pell, Old Breeder Moss) who sell a mystery Egg (₽900/1400/2400, better odds of a rare tier the more it costs) or swap one of your Eggs for a fresh one, once each per stage; you carry at most 4 Eggs. Data in `plazaLife.json` `eggTraders` and `eggs.json` origins. No save change.
