- Rival progression & world recognition (Expanded Systems M11 + M19: §5, §8.3-8.5; no save bump, all new fields optional). **One source of truth:** `core/rivals/progression.ts`. Every rival change is a `rivalProgress` domain event (kinds: unseenWin, unseenLoss, sitOut, badge, rank, beatPlayer, lostToPlayer, narrativeWin, beatRival, lostToRival, setPace) and `emit` applies it to `rival.world` (lead in stages, badges this region, regions done, wins, defeats, fame, last 8 milestones). `rivalStatus` gives Traveling/Ahead/Equal/Behind/Gym Challenger/Gym Victor/Champion Candidate/Champion/Missing/Defeated. M12 narrative defeats record as `narrativeWin` (hooks.ts) and NPCs mention them; M13 replays emit `setPace` and set `FALSE_PROTAGONIST_FLAG` (`core/rivals/recognition.ts`). World (`recognition.ts`, `data/rivalRecognition.json`): ordinary trainers sometimes open with a line about the rival's news or your title (30%, hashed); posters, headlines, Champion board and rival leaderboard on the Story screen, newest headline in the map's notice corner; one rival sighting per stage at most (40%, fits the status; the discarded-item sighting gives a Super Potion). Signature teams (`signatureTeam.ts`): each rival's first Pokémon is its ace (`rival.ace`), evolves with it and is sent out last, with an ace intro and a defeat line. Rivalries (`rivalries.ts`): enemies duel off-screen (30%), gym races, spotlight steals, obsession after 2 duel losses (+5 ambition), and an ally or the foe's enemy joins forces with you once against a far-ahead rival (heals your team). Protagonist Effect: Nobody → Rising Star → Regional Challenger → Rival's Nightmare → People's Champion → The Protagonist → The Unwritten Legend (False Protagonist from the M13 flag); announced once in the chronicle, never a gameplay bonus. Archived rivals keep a `world` summary for the Hall of Fame (M18). Tests `tests/unit/rivalProgression.test.ts`. Limited: recognition is text only (no poster art yet; M16 presentation); NPCs speak through trainer openings, there are no town NPCs.
