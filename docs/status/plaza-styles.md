Rules, menus and prices are untouched. Tests `tests/unit/plazaStyles.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/plaza.spec.ts` (every style). Limited: you still hop between districts with the arrow keys instead of walking freely (free walking could reuse Hub Town's overworld movement later).
