- Trainer Plaza (APPROVED.md §11; `core/plaza/`, data `src/data/plaza.json`): every stage map gets a plaza stop (`plaza` side stop) on **every node of its middle column**, so any route meets it (`placePlaza`, no random numbers; not on the league map). One plaza per stage: its stock, visitors, rumours and event come from the run seed, region and stage, so a reload looks the same and runs differ. Menu on the map (`MapScene.openPlaza`): **Merchant stalls** (6 specialist stalls, 3-4 open per visit, rarer stock with reputation, regular discounts), **Oddities Dealer** (clues instead of names until Intelligence 4+), **Specialist traders** (10 swaps by species/type/stage/weight/level; some unlock by errand), **Trade board** (notices: show a type, hand over an item, prove a counter, give a Pokémon), **Rumour board** (real stops ahead as firsthand/secondhand/hearsay, hearsay can be wrong; paid exact tips reveal "?" stops or the gym team), **Battle Club** (Friendly match, 3-match streak, themed cup, challenge team; friendly losses revive the team and never wipe; `battle.event.eventId = "plaza:club"`), **Errands** (14 jobs from the regulars; counter errands count from when you took them and carry across stages), **Back alley** (opens at reputation 8, a flag, low Good or Intelligence 6; risky deals), **Services** (nurse once, groomer, appraisal desk, team advice; each limited so heal stops stay useful), **People** (14 regulars with friendship lines and discounts, factions with rivals, the rival's visit, past enemies with a rematch, event handouts, party reactions) and a **Caravan** now and then. Plaza events: festival, tournament, auction, emergency, celebrity, evacuation. Reputation 0-60 with titles Stranger to Plaza Legend. Run state `plaza` (rep, friends, flags, errands, done, used, foes, club) has defaults, so no save version bump. Playtest bot (`--sides`) visits it. Tests `tests/unit/plaza.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/plaza.spec.ts`. Limited: no level-restricted club format; the caravan follow-up is one thank-you parcel at the next plaza (not a multi-node event); rival and old-foe rematches use a generated team (the rival's real team is not reused); rumours do not yet point at roaming Legendaries or gang hideouts as real map stops.
