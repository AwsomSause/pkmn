- Pokémon rivalry kinds (left-out 5.4, Techno §1; `core/story/rivalryKinds.ts`): a rivalry record now has a tone. Bitter: your Pokémon fell to a foe at 25% HP or less, and meeting that species again (lead or switch-in) gives "It Hasn't Forgotten That Loss!" (Atk +1). One-Sided: it fell to a foe 5+ levels higher; knocking out that species later makes it Mutual ("Now It's a Real Rivalry!", +10 friendship, not rarity-gated). Friendly: a Friendly Competition teammate tagging in while the other can still fight gives "Friendly Rivals Tag In!" (Atk +1). Generational: a returning rival whose Pokémon evolved from one it brought last time gives "It Has Grown Since Last Time!". The tone is optional on the record (no save bump). Tests `tests/unit/rivalryKinds.test.ts`. Limited: no duo attack move; Bitter's reckless drawback is not added (one effect per moment).
