# Whole-Game Events F: Secrets, Extremely Rare Events and Meta Moments (GWE-F01 to F36)

Hidden, quiet, sometimes strange. Most are `XR` (once in hundreds of hours). Many use real-world clock facts (date, time of day, play count), which must be read once and cached in the seed so tests stay deterministic. Legend as file A.

---

## F1. Hidden Content Unlocks

**GWE-F01 The Door That Isn't There.** *Trigger:* tap the same Hub wall tile 100 times over many sessions. A door appears into a "Backstage" area with developer-style notes. *Rar/Cx:* XR/S.

**GWE-F02 Konami Echo.** Entering a classic code on the title screen plays a short retro jingle and unlocks a joke cosmetic. *Rar/Cx:* XR/S.

**GWE-F03 The 1,000th Run Glitch.** A staged "glitch" where the screen scrambles then reveals a secret mini-boss "MissingNo.-like" original creature, safe to fight. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-F04 Archive Zero.** Finish a run with every Pokémon at level 1 to unlock a hidden "Archive" of original prototypes. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-F05 Moon Phase Locked Door.** A Hub door opens only during a specific real moon phase; behind it is a one-time gift. *Rar/Cx:* XR/S.

**GWE-F06 Cipher Week.** NPC dialogue slowly picks up a code; deciphering it points to a hidden stop whose reward is a title. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-F07 The Library's Last Shelf.** A shelf shows blank books; they fill with text for your own unique history, which only appears after 500 runs. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-F08 Dream Sequence.** After the credits, a short surreal dream scene reflects your party's memories; waking plays an altered Hub for one session. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-F09 A Secret Only Shinies See.** A shiny Pokémon on your team hears something others can't: unique whispered dialogue in a special location. *Rar/Cx:* XR/S.

**GWE-F10 Your Birthday.** If you enter your birthday in settings, the world celebrates it (cake, guests, an optional rival gift). Stored locally only. *Rar/Cx:* C/S.

## F2. Rare World Moments

**GWE-F11 The Silent Day.** Music and sound effects vanish for a day; dialogue boxes tell the story of quiet. The Hub is peaceful. *Rar/Cx:* XR/S.

**GWE-F12 Every Trainer Is Your Rival.** For one session all trainers use your rival's attitude and a tiny piece of their team; funny and tense. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-F13 The Mirror World Opens.** A mirror portal swaps the map left-to-right and flips all dialogue to mirror text for a run. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-F14 Ten Thousand Steps.** Walking through 10,000 map steps in total unlocks a pedometer reward, fewer random encounters for a while. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-F15 Wandering Merchant of Lost Things.** Once per hundred runs a merchant sells every item you ever discarded or lost, bundled together. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-F16 Time Capsule.** On profile creation you may bury a capsule; years later (real time) it is dug up with a letter from your past self. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-F17 The Great Silence of the Legendaries.** All legends are missing for a month; later they return in groups, shaped by events during the silence. *Rar/Cx:* XR/L.

**GWE-F18 Night of a Thousand Lanterns.** Once a year lanterns float up from every route; making a wish picks a random modifier for your next run. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-F19 The Perfect Run Phantom.** After a perfect run, a spectral version of that run appears and replays; if you beat it on points you gain a title. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-F20 Abandoned Save.** An NPC shows you a save that looks like yours but ruined; fixing it with a puzzle gives a beautiful relic. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-F21 Eclipse of the Hub.** The Hub darkens in a special unrepeatable animation; Pokémon gather around you quietly. *Rar/Cx:* XR/S.

**GWE-F22 The Wishing Fountain Overflows.** After 1,000 coins thrown into the Hub fountain it overflows, producing a unique Pokémon egg. *Rar/Cx:* R/S.

**GWE-F23 The Lost Gym.** A gym that never existed appears with a never-seen leader and a never-seen type mix. Disappears after one challenge. *Rar/Cx:* XR/L.

**GWE-F24 Weekend of the Wild.** All trainers vanish: only wild encounters, huge pools. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

## F3. Meta and Fourth-Wall Moments

**GWE-F25 The Developer's Note.** After a long absence, a polite note from "the developer" appears, thanking you with a gift and a short cheeky joke about bugs. *Rar/Cx:* XR/S.

**GWE-F26 Credits Roll Mid-Run.** Rarely, credits roll over a mid-run map (the game "breaks" then returns), giving the player a small reward for patience. *Rar/Cx:* XR/S.

**GWE-F27 The Game Remembers Your Playtime.** At exactly 100 hours the Hub's old man says "you've spent a hundred hours here" and gives a complimentary mug. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-F28 The Save File Speaks.** If a save recovery happens, the recovered data presents itself as a character apologising and helping fix it. *Rar/Cx:* C/S. *Ties:* save recovery.

**GWE-F29 Rival Reads the Patch Notes.** After an update, the rival comments on new features in-world. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-F30 Cheater's Nightmare.** Using debug settings plants a ghostly "Cheater" Pokémon in the next run, harmless but comedic. *Rar/Cx:* XR/S.

**GWE-F31 The Final Boss Takes a Day Off.** On rare days the final boss is on holiday and a sleepy substitute fights instead. *Rar/Cx:* XR/S.

**GWE-F32 Pokédex Completion Ceremony.** A grand fireworks parade named after your completion; unlocks the Hub's "Master Study". *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-F33 Loading Screen Trivia Becomes Real.** One loading tip becomes a real event for a day (e.g. "Slowpoke tails sell for a lot!"). *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-F34 The Fourth Starter.** After finishing all three starters' campaigns a lab assistant reveals a fourth cached starter that never existed, tied to your history. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-F35 Real-Time Clock Surprise.** Playing at exactly midnight on New Year's Eve triggers a hidden countdown scene with every NPC you know. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-F36 The World Without You.** For one session the game shows the world as it would be if you never played: NPCs speaking about a hero who never came. Powerful, bittersweet, with a gift for coming back. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.
