# Interactions 05: Hidden, ultra-specific, emotional and meta moments (I-441 to I-540)

Part 5 of 5. Design ideas only. Format: **Name** — *What* · *How* · *Why* · `Cx` (S/M/L) · `Sys`. These are the "only under extremely specific circumstances" entries: each names the exact trigger. Tones mixed on purpose: funny, awkward, tense, mysterious, heartbreaking. Triggers use data the game already tracks (personality, quirk, chemistry stage, faint history, memories log, Legacy runs, Album flags).
