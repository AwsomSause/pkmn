# Run Events 3: Echoes of Previous Runs and Legacy (RE-C01 to RE-C60)

Design only, nothing built. Part of the run-event library. Rarity C/U/R/VR/M, Size S/M/L/XL.
These read the profile's **legacy run records** (`profile.legacyRuns`: team, outcome, rival, region, titles, Hall of Fame, nemeses beaten, memories). Rule: they only reference facts that really happened, are skipped on a first run, and never change a past record.

**RE-C01 The Ghost of Your Last Team** · U · M
Trigger: at least one earlier run recorded. Scene: a faint copy of your previous team stands in the road. Outcomes: battle it (a team with last run's species at your current level; win = XP + "Beat Yourself" tally), walk past (nothing), join it (swap one member for a recorded one for this stage). Reward/Cost: XP. Variations: uses the most recent or the best run.

**RE-C02 A Statue of Your Champion** · R · M
Trigger: you won the Hall of Fame at least once. Scene: a plinth with your past champion's ace. Outcomes: salute (Bond +1), ask it a favour (the ace's species appears as a wild gift), vandalise (rival laughs). Reward/Cost: gift. Variations: statue shows the winning date.

**RE-C03 Letters From the Run That Ended** · U · S
Trigger: last run ended in defeat. Scene: a letter written by the old party. Outcomes: read (advice hint about the node that killed it), burn, answer. Reward/Cost: hint. Variations: text quotes the final fight.

**RE-C04 The Rematch Nobody Asked For** · R · M
Trigger: you lost to a specific boss. Scene: that boss's lieutenant "remembers" you. Outcomes: accept the rematch (pre-boss practice battle, rewards a strategy hint), decline, ask for a tip. Reward/Cost: hint. Variations: boss-specific line.

**RE-C05 Veteran Trainer Recognises Your Species** · C · S
Trigger: you used the same starter in 2+ runs. Scene: an NPC says "you again". Outcomes: smile (Bond +1), brag (rival notices), confide (a tip about starter's line). Reward/Cost: tip. Variations: gives the starter a title.
