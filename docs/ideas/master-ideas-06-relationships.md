# Master Idea Library 06: Relationships, Rivalries, Friendships & Chemistry (RL-001 to RL-080)

Legend and fields as in file 01. Design only. Builds on the Living Rival, Bond, Nemesis and relationship-path work already in the game.

## A. Pokémon-to-Pokémon chemistry

**RL-001 Chemistry Archetypes** — Pairs fall into archetypes (Best Friends, Bickering Siblings, Mentor-Pupil, Rivals, Odd Couple, Quiet Admirers) that colour all banter and give distinct perks. *Trigger:* time together. *Feel:* soap-opera depth. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* Bond, dialogue. *Balance:* all archetypes viable.

**RL-002 Shared Meal Rituals** — Sharing berries at rest builds chemistry; who gets the last berry is a tiny decision with consequences. *Trigger:* rest. *Feel:* intimate. *Rarity:* Common. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* berries, rest. *Balance:* tiny.

**RL-003 Rivalry Between Teammates** — Two Pokémon of similar role compete for spotlight; battle performance feeds the rivalry; resolved by a face-off event. *Trigger:* similar roles. *Feel:* team drama. *Rarity:* Uncommon. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* mood. *Balance:* resolution perk.

**RL-004 Protector Bonds** — Strong Pokémon "adopt" weak ones, taking hits for them; the weak one levels faster. *Trigger:* mismatched pair. *Feel:* heartwarming. *Rarity:* Uncommon. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* doubles. *Balance:* modest.

**RL-005 Love Triangles** — Three party members tangle in affection; handling it affects morale (silly and sweet, not romantic for humans). *Trigger:* high chemistry. *Feel:* funny. *Rarity:* Rare. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* chemistry. *Balance:* flavour.

**RL-006 Pair Moves Unlocked By Friendship** — Best friends unlock a unique pair move. *Trigger:* chemistry max. *Feel:* reward. *Rarity:* Rare. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* doubles. *Balance:* limited.

**RL-007 Estranged Friends** — A fight (mid-run event) splits friends; they won't share a battle until you mediate. *Trigger:* event. *Feel:* heartache. *Rarity:* Uncommon. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* events. *Balance:* resolvable.

**RL-008 Pack Hierarchies** — Same-species groups form pecking orders; promotion changes who leads. *Trigger:* group. *Feel:* naturalism. *Rarity:* Rare. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* chemistry. *Balance:* flavour.

**RL-009 Evolution Envy** — A friend evolving may cause a pause; another evolves out of solidarity. *Trigger:* evolution. *Feel:* odd. *Rarity:* Rare. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* evolution. *Balance:* none.

**RL-010 Lullaby Pairs** — Certain pairs (Jigglypuff and Snorlax) soothe each other for rest bonuses. *Trigger:* pair present. *Feel:* species lore. *Rarity:* Species. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* rest. *Balance:* small.
