# Whole-Game Events C: Events Driven by Your History (GWE-C01 to C36)

The world reads `profile.legacyRuns`, Hall of Fame, nemeses, memories and tallies and **answers back**. All are derived from saved data (no new RNG). Legend as file A.

---

## C1. The World Remembers Achievements

**GWE-C01 Statue in the Square.** *Trigger:* first Champion win. *Scope:* permanent. A statue of your Ace appears in a town; each later win adds a wreath. A defeat with that Ace leaves the statue cracked until you win again. *Rar/Cx:* C/S.

**GWE-C02 Street Named After You.** *Trigger:* 25 runs. A map route is renamed after your trainer name. NPCs say "Down [Name] Road". *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-C03 Children Play As You.** *Trigger:* 10 wins with the same starter. Kids in towns play "[Name] and [Starter]". Talking to them gives pointers they invented. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-C04 A Book About You.** *Trigger:* 50 runs. A bookstore stocks an unauthorised biography with chapters drawn from your Chronicle; reading unlocks a "tell the truth" choice to correct one error for a small reward. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-C05 The Documentary.** *Trigger:* a flawless run. A film crew follows one later run; they ask to film pivotal fights and your team reacts to being watched (+focus, -candid dialogue). Audience score sets a pay. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-C06 Hall of Fame Tour.** *Trigger:* 10 Hall of Fame teams. Tourists visit the Hub and ask for team stories; your answers shift your reputation score. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-C07 Anniversary of Your First Win.** *Trigger:* calendar date of your first Champion clear. The day, a message arrives from the original Champion NPC with a gift and a rematch. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-C08 The Trophy Case Burglar.** *Trigger:* many keepsakes in the loft. A thief tries to steal one on a random night; a Hub defence minigame decides if you keep it. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-C09 Sponsored by Your Fame.** *Trigger:* Fame tier reached. Brands offer deals tied to your style (e.g. potions company wants low-damage wins). Fulfilling gives income, breaking costs. *Rar/Cx:* U/M. *Ties:* sponsors in rivalWorld.

**GWE-C10 Your Handwriting Becomes a Font.** *Trigger:* 100 runs. The UI offers a hand-drawn font that matches your nickname history. *Rar/Cx:* R/S. *Ties:* cosmetics.

## C2. The World Remembers Failures

**GWE-C11 The Graveyard Garden.** *Trigger:* 10 run wipes. A quiet garden holds a stone per wipe with your team's final line. Visiting yields comfort buffs. *Rar/Cx:* U/S. *Ties:* narrative defeat.

**GWE-C12 Underdog Following.** *Trigger:* 5 losses in a row. A fan club appears and cheers: a tiny bonus to morale for the next run, vanishing at a win. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-C13 The Rematch Day.** *Trigger:* you lost to a boss 3 times. The boss sends a letter inviting a "friendly" rematch with their weakest team, a gift if you win. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-C14 Tabloid Rumours.** *Trigger:* a very public loss. Newspapers print rumours of retirement; NPCs treat you as washed-up until you win once. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-C15 The Lost Item Shop.** *Trigger:* 20 items lost to defeat. A shop sells your old items back at a discount, the originals with marks of wear. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-C16 Haunting Echo.** *Trigger:* the same Pokémon fainted 5 times. The Pokémon begins to hesitate at low HP (extra dialogue) until you pass a Reassurance event. *Rar/Cx:* U/M. *Ties:* party life.

**GWE-C17 Wanted Poster.** *Trigger:* defeating a faction repeatedly. Wanted posters of you appear; faction scouts ambush in certain stops. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-C18 Redemption Arc.** *Trigger:* a rival beaten you 10 times. A "Redemption Run" modifier lets you take them on at fair terms with a bonus for each previous loss. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

## C3. Callbacks to Specific Past Runs

**GWE-C19 Ghost of a Past Team.** *Trigger:* a Legacy run with a famous team. A phantom of that team appears as an optional boss on that run's seed (their moveset from the record). *Rar/Cx:* R/L. *Ties:* legacyRuns, timeline replays.

**GWE-C20 The Seed Pilgrimage.** *Trigger:* a seed you won with perfectly. A shrine appears; replaying that seed gives a golden cosmetic. *Rar/Cx:* U/M. *Ties:* seedShelf.

**GWE-C21 Descendants.** *Trigger:* a Pokémon with 5+ runs retires. Its offspring egg is offered at the Hub, inheriting a move and a note about its parent's personality. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-C22 The Reunion Dinner.** *Trigger:* 3 previous teams retired. At year's end, all retired Pokémon attend a dinner scene. Small choices decide who sits next to whom, shaping friendships. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

**GWE-C23 Old Rival Returns After Years.** *Trigger:* 60 real days since last meeting. They return with a changed team and a changed attitude depending on how you left things. *Rar/Cx:* R/M. *Ties:* rival world.

**GWE-C24 Your Old Nickname.** *Trigger:* renamed a Pokémon 3 times. NPCs from earlier runs call your Pokémon by an earlier nickname, creating confusion gags and one touching event. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-C25 The Same Mistake Twice.** *Trigger:* a decision repeated and failed 3 times. An NPC calls it out kindly and sells a hint item. *Rar/Cx:* C/S.

**GWE-C26 Nemesis Museum.** *Trigger:* 5 nemeses slain. The museum stages a nemesis wing; each exhibit has a mini quiz, with rewards for correct answers about how they fell. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-C27 Memory Lane Festival.** *Trigger:* 50 memories recorded. An annual festival replays six random memories in a parade. *Rar/Cx:* U/M. *Ties:* memories.

**GWE-C28 What If? Parade.** *Trigger:* any run where an alternate choice was available. Time-travel entertainers let you view one "what if" branch. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

## C4. Statistics and Milestones as Events

**GWE-C29 1,000th Battle.** A confetti scene, a one-off Pokémon gift, and a wall plaque. *Rar/Cx:* C/S.

**GWE-C30 100th Shiny Anniversary.** A special "Shiny Day" where all wild shiny odds are visible as a hint meter. *Rar/Cx:* XR/S.

**GWE-C31 Billionaire's Offer.** *Trigger:* lifetime money hoarded. A rich NPC proposes buying out your savings for a unique title; refusing offers a quest. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-C32 The Perfect Month.** *Trigger:* play every day for 30 days. Streak rewards: a month-long visible calendar with a special, silly pet. *Rar/Cx:* U/S. *Ties:* daily run.

**GWE-C33 The Long Absence.** *Trigger:* away 60 days. The Hub is dusty; NPCs welcome you back; a "Return Pack" offers a catch-up bundle, free of shame. *Rar/Cx:* C/S.

**GWE-C34 Style Awards.** *Trigger:* run summaries accumulate. The "Playstyle Awards" ceremony names you for your most-used style (Sweeper, Staller, Gambler) and sets next month's cosmetic. *Rar/Cx:* U/M. *Ties:* playstyle titles.

**GWE-C35 Generational Handover.** *Trigger:* 100 runs. The game offers to turn your profile into a "Master" mentor NPC for a fresh profile (a new-game plus without losing the old data). *Rar/Cx:* XR/XL.

**GWE-C36 The Last Page.** *Trigger:* all achievements. A closing credits roll that references your real stats; afterwards the world "continues" with new secrets accessible only to completionists. *Rar/Cx:* XR/L.
