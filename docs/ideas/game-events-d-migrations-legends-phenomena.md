# Whole-Game Events D: Migrations, Legends, Phenomena and Cycles (GWE-D01 to D36)

Mostly date-seeded or tally-seeded so they work offline. Distinct from the one-line weeks in `ideas/game-events.md`: these have **memory** (they escalate or decay over runs). Legend as file A.

---

## D1. Migrations

**GWE-D01 The Great Butterfree Migration.** Each autumn a drifting cloud of Butterfree crosses the map; defeating none of them (just walking through) earns a petal trail reward, defeating many lowers next year's migration size. *Scope:* 1 week/yr, with yearly memory. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-D02 Salmon Run.** Water Pokémon swim upstream; each route stop shows moving fish silhouettes; Bear-type predators gather. A "feed vs catch" choice flips next season's Water pool. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-D03 Wild Pokémon Move South.** Over the winter, Ice/Flying types shift to southern regions. Visible arrows on the world map. *Rar/Cx:* C/S.

**GWE-D04 Nesting Season.** Egg nests appear on maps; leaving them alone grows wild populations, taking eggs gives hatching bonuses but angers parents (defensive wild fights). *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-D05 The Wandering Herd.** A herd of one species roams between regions across runs; it appears wherever you finished your last run. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-D06 Stormbird Season.** Flying types gather before storms, boosting the Flying wild pool for exactly the runs preceding a Weather Event. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-D07 Bug Hatch Swarm.** Every N runs bugs hatch en masse; stops are packed with low-level Bugs that give small XP chains. Beware of the one Heracross. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-D08 Ghost Parade of the Lost.** Unclaimed Pokémon from deleted or abandoned runs drift as a ghost parade; touching one lets you adopt it with a tragic backstory. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-D09 Fossil Seasons.** Erosion unearths fossils; each fossil type has its own season. A museum quest requires finding the full set. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-D10 Starlit Pilgrimage.** Fairy and Psychic types travel to a shrine on meteor nights; shrines grant wishes (a single guaranteed outcome) to those who bring an offering. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

## D2. Legendary Activity

**GWE-D11 The Roaming Legend Calendar.** A different legend roams each month and leaves hints (footprints, weather). Its chase builds across runs: each sighting grows your catch odds up to a cap. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

**GWE-D12 Legend Tantrum.** If you farm legends too often a guardian grows annoyed and legends vanish for 5 runs; apologising (gift) ends it. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-D13 Twin Legends, One Season.** Two legends of opposite types alternate; whichever you meet first shapes the next 10 runs' weather. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-D14 Legend Hatchlings.** A legend's egg is discovered; it hatches over 3 months of runs; contributions of warmth (friendship events) speed it. *Rar/Cx:* XR/L.

**GWE-D15 Fallen Legend.** A legend arrives weakened (HP bar already low, stat-lowered) and wild bosses harass it. Protect it to earn an ally for one run. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-D16 Legends in Disguise.** Starting a certain date, a legend wanders as a plain Pokémon; the shiny-like sparkle hints but never confirms. Catching it unprepared reveals it. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-D17 Legend Council.** Every 100 runs, legends meet and discuss your region; the outcome (a vote tally, partially tied to your actions) changes boss rosters. *Rar/Cx:* XR/L.

**GWE-D18 The Rival Legend.** Your rival catches a legend; a Hub rumour suggests they will use it; the seed ensures it appears in the final fight if you let them. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-D19 Legend's Debt.** A legend you spared returns a favour years later, intervening at a pivotal fight in a future run. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-D20 Mythical Stamp Rally.** Collect stamps from 12 shrines across runs to summon a mythical on one special day. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

## D3. Natural and Cosmic Phenomena

**GWE-D21 Aurora Season.** Auroras light the sky; for a week Psychic and Ice moves glow and are slightly stronger; photographs unlock a gallery cosmetic. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-D22 Double Rainbow.** A once-in-a-while rainbow after rain spawns a golden-tinted rare wild. *Rar/Cx:* XR/S.

**GWE-D23 Solar Flare.** Electric Pokémon overcharge: shocks fire randomly and device-themed items malfunction. Fun but risky. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-D24 Comet Pass.** A comet passes once every 76 days; all Dragon encounters have increased appearance and the comet shard item exists for that run only. *Rar/Cx:* R/S.

**GWE-D25 Tidal Wave Warning.** A looming wave forces a run-long countdown; surviving it grants a Water-type blessing. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-D26 Drought.** Water stops dry up for 3 runs; Grass wild pool shrinks; fire spreads. Rain dance rituals end it. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-D27 Earthquake Swarm.** Small tremors shuffle one map stop each battle; a seismologist NPC gives a prediction tool afterward. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-D28 Blue Moon.** The second full moon in a month: every moon-themed move gains an extra effect for that run; one special boss appears only then. *Rar/Cx:* XR/S.

**GWE-D29 The Day the Sun Didn't Rise.** One day, the sun stays hidden; all fights are dark/night with unique Ghost/Dark mechanics. The next day brings relief. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-D30 Meteor Shower Wishes.** On shower nights tap three falling stars for small boons; the last star is a Wishing Star item. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-D31 Magnetic Storm.** Steel/Electric types become overly attracted to each other: forced doubles pairings. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-D32 Volcanic Ash Winter.** After a volcano event, ash dims all colours and lowers Grass growth for a month; Rock-types thrive. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-D33 Poison Mist Drift.** A poison mist floats across the map between runs; where it lands decides a Poison outbreak. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-D34 Time Slips.** For a day, minute-long jumps between past and future seeds; items in the bag randomly appear aged or fresh. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

**GWE-D35 Pollen Season.** Grass types spread pollen; allergy status effects, but also double berry drops. *Rar/Cx:* C/S.

**GWE-D36 Tidal Bloom.** Luminescent algae turns beaches into light shows; night routes are safer and prettier. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.
