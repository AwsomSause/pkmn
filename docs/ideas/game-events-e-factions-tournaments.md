# Whole-Game Events E: Factions, Tournaments, Invasions and Economies (GWE-E01 to E36)

Persistent power maps and competitions. Faction standing lives in the world record and extends `rivalWorld.json` factions/sponsors/debts. Legend as file A.

---

## E1. Faction Conflicts and Invasions

**GWE-E01 Front Lines.** Two factions fight over map regions; each run you see the frontier shift based on aggregate player wins against each. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

**GWE-E02 Coup at the League.** A faction attempts a takeover; the league's boss roster changes to the faction's team for a month unless you win enough "defence" battles. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

**GWE-E03 Occupation.** A faction occupies a region; all shops use their stock and gym leaders are replaced by admins. Liberation takes multi-run "resistance" tasks. *Rar/Cx:* R/XL.

**GWE-E04 Smuggling Ring.** An underworld ring moves contraband Pokémon between towns; busting it pays and unlocks forbidden-trade events. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-E05 The Sneak Attack on the Hub.** Once per ~100 runs raiders attack your Hub; a defensive battle uses your *retired* Pokémon as defenders. *Rar/Cx:* XR/L. *Ties:* retired roster.

**GWE-E06 Peace Treaty Summit.** Two enemy factions meet; your past choices decide who is invited; the summit's outcome affects four months of events. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

**GWE-E07 Faction Tithe.** A dominant faction taxes shops in its territory; paying keeps peace, refusing triggers ambushes. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-E08 Infiltrator Among Us.** A faction spy joins an NPC group in the Hub; hints lead to spotting them; wrong accusations hurt trust. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-E09 Propaganda Posters.** Factions plaster posters that subtly alter trainer dialogue; tearing them down yields minor rewards and enemies. *Rar/Cx:* C/S.

**GWE-E10 Mercenary Season.** Free-lance trainers offer to battle alongside you for pay; hired mercs betray if a faction offers more. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-E11 Siege of the Gym.** A gym is besieged; you choose to break the siege or help the defenders hold; outcomes differ for leaders' loyalty. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-E12 Counter-Revolution.** After a faction falls, a splinter group forms with a darker leader. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-E13 War Orphans.** Pokémon left behind by war look for new trainers; adopting them gives inheritable "scar" traits that matter in story. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-E14 Faction Fashion.** Faction colours become trending; wearing them yields bonuses and enemies in equal measure. *Rar/Cx:* C/S. *Ties:* trainer cosmetics.

**GWE-E15 Double Agent Quest.** Work for two factions at once; either can expose you; the final judgement decides trust. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

## E2. Tournaments and Championships

**GWE-E16 The World Cup of Types.** Every four years (calendar) types compete as national teams; your runs score points for the type you used most. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-E17 Gauntlet of Retired Champions.** A tournament where the champions are your own old Legacy teams in bracket order; you can bet on the winner. *Rar/Cx:* R/L. *Ties:* legacyRuns.

**GWE-E18 Sudden Death Bracket.** Random trainers drawn from your history face one-on-one battles; winners earn a golden ticket. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-E19 Masters Seeding Ceremony.** Your Fame sets your seed in a public bracket against NPC seeds; high seeds get easier early rounds. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-E20 Interregional Cup.** Regions compete; your clears give points to your "home region" (selected at profile creation) and ties are broken by memorability. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-E21 Amateur Hour.** A casual tournament with handicaps: teams of 3, level caps, silly rules (no status moves). Free entry, bragging rights. *Rar/Cx:* C/S.

**GWE-E22 Mirror Match Championship.** You fight a mirror of your own team including the same nicknames. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-E23 The Blindfold Open.** Teams hidden until revealed in fight; shops sell clairvoyance. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-E24 Doubles Dynasty.** A doubles-only championship where partner NPCs are drawn from past rivals. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-E25 Coaches' Cup.** You coach an NPC protégé through a tournament by giving advice between rounds (no direct control). *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

**GWE-E26 Hall of Fame Draft.** A draft where retired champions pick the Pokémon they would use; their picks are displayed. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-E27 Underground Circuit.** A secret circuit unlocked by hearsay in NPC dialogue; entering requires a code deciphered across runs. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-E28 Rain Delay.** A tournament postponed by weather resumes in the next run; incomplete brackets persist. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-E29 Championship Betting.** Place bets on NPC match-ups using in-game currency, shown on the Hub board; outcomes seeded by date. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-E30 Retirement Match.** An NPC legend announces a final bout and you can be their last opponent; winning gives their signature move. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

## E3. Economy Events

**GWE-E31 Market Crash.** Item prices swing wildly for a week; sell/buy timing becomes a minigame. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-E32 Gold Rush.** A mine yields gold; stops overflow with prospectors and claim jumpers. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-E33 Inflation Month.** Everything costs more, but rewards scale; pure score-chasers thrive. *Rar/Cx:* C/S.

**GWE-E34 Monopoly Wars.** Shop chains compete, giving coupons to loyal customers. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-E35 Collector's Auction.** Rare items (including ones you donated) are auctioned; bidding uses story-based currency like memories. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

**GWE-E36 Barter Days.** Money is banned; only item-for-item trades happen at shops, with a lore reason. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.
