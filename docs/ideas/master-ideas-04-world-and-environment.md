# Master Idea Library 04: World-Building & Environmental Storytelling (WD-001 to WD-080)

Legend and fields as in file 01. Design only.

## A. Places with history

**WD-001 Layered Ruins** — Ruin nodes have three visible strata (modern litter, old town, ancient carvings); digging reveals deeper layers with better loot but waking older things. *Trigger:* ruin node. *Feel:* archaeological risk. *Rarity:* Uncommon. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* map events. *Balance:* depth ties to danger.

**WD-002 Abandoned Gym Husks** — Gyms from earlier runs' defeated leaders sit as ruins with graffiti telling a leader's fate; you can salvage a trophy. *Trigger:* postgame/Legacy. *Feel:* world remembers. *Rarity:* Rare. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* Legacy. *Balance:* cosmetic plus small trophy.

**WD-003 Memorial Wall** — A town wall lists fallen Pokémon from all your runs by name with epitaphs. Stand before it to trigger a quiet event. *Trigger:* Hub. *Feel:* gravity. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* permadeath log. *Balance:* none.

**WD-004 Echo Towns** — Towns repeat key features across runs but drift (the bakery becomes a pharmacy) based on your reputation. *Trigger:* new run. *Feel:* the world continues. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* procedural towns. *Balance:* none.

**WD-005 Lighthouse Keeper** — A lighthouse node has a keeper who logs the passing of every run; reading logs reveals hints about rare events. *Trigger:* coastal node. *Feel:* lore anchor. *Rarity:* Uncommon. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* event hints. *Balance:* hints vague.

**WD-006 Forgotten Trainer Schools** — Derelict academies with training dummies; each holds a lesson that grants a small skill. *Trigger:* node. *Feel:* learning. *Rarity:* Rare. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* skills. *Balance:* one lesson per run.

**WD-007 Standing Stones Network** — Stones across regions link; activating several reveals a hidden location. *Trigger:* exploring. *Feel:* puzzle meta. *Rarity:* Rare. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* map meta. *Balance:* cosmetic plus unlock.

**WD-008 Battle Scars On Terrain** — Locations of boss fights keep visible damage in later runs (scorched crater). *Trigger:* boss win. *Feel:* continuity. *Rarity:* Long-term. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* map art. *Balance:* none.

**WD-009 Ghost Towns** — Towns erased by past events appear with ghost NPCs who tell stories and trade unique items. *Trigger:* rare. *Feel:* spooky. *Rarity:* Rare. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* events. *Balance:* varies.

**WD-010 Overgrown Facilities** — Abandoned labs overtaken by Grass types, with logs showing what went wrong. *Trigger:* node. *Feel:* storytelling by scenery. *Rarity:* Uncommon. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* lore. *Balance:* risk reward.

## B. Ecology and living systems

**WD-011 Food Webs** — Wild spawns follow a predator-prey graph; removing prey (catching all Rattata) draws in predators next visit. *Trigger:* over-harvest. *Feel:* consequence. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* encounter tables. *Balance:* soft effects.

**WD-012 Migration Seasons** — Flocks migrate on schedule; node tables shift by calendar. *Trigger:* date. *Feel:* living calendar. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* weekly events. *Balance:* none.

**WD-013 Nesting Sites** — Visible nests on maps with eggs; take one and parents may hunt you, leave and they become allies later. *Trigger:* node. *Feel:* moral choice. *Rarity:* Uncommon. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* eggs. *Balance:* tradeoff.

**WD-014 Keystone Species** — A keystone Pokémon in each biome (Slowbro in swamp); catching it collapses the local ecosystem. *Trigger:* catch. *Feel:* thoughtful consequence. *Rarity:* Rare. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* biome tables. *Balance:* opt-in.

**WD-015 Territorial Disputes** — Two wild species fight over a node; choose a side to gain an ally. *Trigger:* node. *Feel:* choices. *Rarity:* Uncommon. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* events. *Balance:* reward both.

**WD-016 Disease Outbreaks** — A region suffers an outbreak affecting wild spawns (Pokérus-like), which can spread to your team. *Trigger:* rare. *Feel:* tension. *Rarity:* Rare. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* statuses. *Balance:* controllable.

**WD-017 Seed Dispersal** — Berries you eat grow at that node later, creating your own berry trail across runs. *Trigger:* eat berry. *Feel:* legacy. *Rarity:* Common. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* berries. *Balance:* low yield.

**WD-018 Animal Tracks** — Footprints on maps hint at which wild Pokémon is nearby; read them to choose encounters. *Trigger:* node. *Feel:* detective. *Rarity:* Common. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* encounter preview. *Balance:* accuracy 80%.


**WD-020 Moon Phases** — Lunar phases affect Pokémon (Clefairy dance at full moon); calendar-aware. *Trigger:* date. *Feel:* mystic. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* weekly events. *Balance:* small.
