# Whole-Game Events B: Multi-Stage Storylines and Campaigns (GWE-B01 to B36)

Each is a **chain**: stages advance across runs (a stage needs a run played, not one battle), stored in `world.chains[id] = {stage, flags}`. Chains never block normal play; they add optional stops, letters and rewards. Legend as in file A. Stage lists use `S1 > S2 > ...`.

---

## B1. Mystery Chains

**GWE-B01 The Lost Researcher.** S1 a torn field note drops at a ruin > S2 find her camp (empty) > S3 her Pokémon waits at a Centre, won't leave > S4 follow it to her hidden lab > S5 she's alive, trapped in a time loop of your runs. *Pay:* Time-themed Pokémon ally, Researcher title. *Rar/Cx:* R/L. *Ties:* Delta Research Week, time-rift events.

**GWE-B02 The Cartographer's Missing Page.** Ten map pages scatter across ten runs; each page, once found, adds a small permanent hint (a stop icon revealed). With all ten you unlock a true secret route. *Rar/Cx:* U/M. *Ties:* hidden stops.

**GWE-B03 The Museum Heist.** A thief steals a museum exhibit between runs. Clues arrive as letters; you pick suspects. Wrong accusations cost a small fee; the right one yields a rematch with a masked trainer who becomes a recurring rival-lite. *Rar/Cx:* R/L. *Ties:* museum, trainer plaza.

**GWE-B04 Signals from Nowhere.** A strange radio tone runs in the Hub; each run adds one decoded character from your own runs' seed digits; the final message tells you where a legend sleeps. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M. *Ties:* strange-signal map event.

**GWE-B05 The Stolen Starter.** Someone steals your region's starter from the lab. Over runs you chase the thief; at the end the "thief" is a Pokémon who wanted to leave. You choose to release it or invite it back. *Pay:* special starter variant. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-B06 The Unsent Letters.** The Hub mailbox fills with letters addressed to a trainer who doesn't exist; reading them across runs reveals a story about a rival from a world that ended, hinting the False Protagonist system. *Rar/Cx:* R/M. *Ties:* False Protagonist, letters.

**GWE-B07 Whispering Forest.** A forest region gets extra quiet each run; wild Pokémon stop attacking first (tame events) until a "Whisperer" boss reveals the cause. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-B08 The Painted Pokémon.** A wandering painter leaves murals in each region; the murals are clues about future boss teams. Complete all murals to commission one for the Hub. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-B09 Echo Cave Mystery.** Voices from past runs echo in a cave: your own old dialogue lines, replayed. Over several runs the echoes begin to answer you differently. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M. *Ties:* memories, chronicle.

**GWE-B10 The Weather Witch.** A wandering witch sells weather in jars; each jar drops a bonus. A multi-run arc ends with the witch's apprentice (a Castform line Pokémon). *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

## B2. Rivalry and Faction Arcs

**GWE-B11 Rival Rewrites the Bracket.** Your rival begins to *cheat* in tournaments; later runs reveal why (debt to a faction). Helping them pays a big long-term loyalty reward. *Rar/Cx:* R/L. *Ties:* rivalWorld debts, sponsors.

**GWE-B12 The Fall of a Gym Leader.** A beloved leader loses their last badge battle and disappears; the story follows them to a tower, where they ask you to fight them at their worst or at their best. *Pay:* leader returns as a coach NPC. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

**GWE-B13 Succession Crisis.** The Champion announces retirement; four candidates (your past rivals) fight for the title across a season of runs. You can back one. *Rar/Cx:* R/XL. *Ties:* Hall of Fame, rival hall.

**GWE-B14 The Defector.** A faction admin appears in your Hub, hunted by their own team. You hide them over multiple runs; they feed you map intel and one day their former boss appears. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

**GWE-B15 The Long Goodbye.** An aging mentor NPC gives you a lesson per run; the final lesson is their retirement gift. *Pay:* a legacy move. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-B16 Sibling Rivalry.** Your rival's younger sibling begins to trail them; each run the kid grows into a threat, eventually a new rival archetype of their own. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-B17 The Friendly Feud.** Two town leaders feud over a festival date; supporting one nudges the town's shop theme permanently. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-B18 Cold Case.** A nemesis who vanished two arcs ago returns *reformed* and asks to join a team. Accepting changes their archetype to Redeemed. *Rar/Cx:* R/M. *Ties:* nemesis.

**GWE-B19 Faction Civil War.** A faction splits into two; each run shows territory shifts on the world map. Winner decides which faction's items are sold. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

**GWE-B20 The Rival's Wedding.** After enough shared runs your rival invites you to a ceremony; the party includes every NPC you've met. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M. *Ties:* Living Rival.

## B3. Legend and Myth Arcs

**GWE-B21 Three Keys of the Deep.** Three keys, one per ocean region, each guarded by its own boss; combine them at the ocean floor for a legendary encounter. Keys persist across runs. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

**GWE-B22 The Sleeping Beast Wakes.** A sleeping legend's breath is heard in the Hub; each run it sleeps shallower; at the end a calm "Awakening" run lets you meet it on its terms. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

**GWE-B23 Mythic Ledger.** Every run you collect "myth shards" from rare events; ten shards forge a mythical encounter. Shard colour depends on your party types. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-B24 The Guardian Pact.** Legendary guardians test your party for fairness: no losses in a region, no revives, no consumables. Passing grants a boon, failing hints at a retry window. *Rar/Cx:* R/M.

**GWE-B25 The Divided Legend.** A legend splits into two forms, one per faction; world state decides which you fight. Reuniting them needs both quests complete. *Rar/Cx:* XR/XL.

**GWE-B26 Dream Eater's Season.** A Pokémon eats your night dreams: for several runs the Hub's sleep screen shows missing scenery. Finding it restores Hub items. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-B27 Moon Orchard.** On full moons, an orchard offers a once-per-moon pick; five picks tell a story of a lost sibling constellation. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-B28 The Tower of Names.** An endless tower records the names of every Pokémon you've ever lost in a run; climbing floors lets you face their "memory battles" for closure and a keepsake. *Rar/Cx:* R/L. *Ties:* memories, defeat.

**GWE-B29 The Last Egg.** A rare egg appears; it will not hatch until a story arc completes across 10 runs, guided by friendship milestones. *Pay:* a unique form. *Rar/Cx:* XR/L.

**GWE-B30 Curse of the Crimson Moon.** A red moon rises once; all shiny odds spike and so do aggressive bosses. A three-run curse then lifts only if you perform a purification ritual. *Rar/Cx:* XR/M.

## B4. Social and Quiet Arcs

**GWE-B31 The Pen Pal.** An anonymous pen pal writes after each run; replies are chosen via short choices. The final letter is a visit. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-B32 The Apprentice.** A kid shows up at the Hub wanting to be your apprentice. Each run you teach a lesson (choice); their team gets built from your decisions, and later they appear as a trainer who fights using your style. *Rar/Cx:* R/L.

**GWE-B33 The Lighthouse Keeper's Diary.** Diary pages arrive about weather and seagulls; there is no combat. A cozy arc that ends with a free Hub decoration. *Rar/Cx:* U/S.

**GWE-B34 Village Recovery.** A destroyed village needs resources; across runs you donate, and it rebuilds visibly into a market town. *Pay:* new shop line. *Rar/Cx:* U/M.

**GWE-B35 The Band Gets Together.** Musicians from different regions join one by one; each run adds an instrument layer to Hub music. A final concert unlocks a battle theme remix. *Rar/Cx:* U/M. *Ties:* music pack.

**GWE-B36 Retirement Home.** Your retired Pokémon begin to appear in a Hub retirement home with their own mini arcs, bickering over stories of "the good run". *Rar/Cx:* R/M. *Ties:* Hall of Fame, party life.
