# Run Events 1: Party Bonds, Personality and Disagreements (RE-A01 to RE-A70)

Design only, nothing built. Part of the 500+ run-event library (index: `run-events-0-index.md`).
Every entry: **Rarity** C common / U uncommon / R rare / VR very rare / M mythic (once in hundreds of hours). **Size** S small charm / M medium / L big / XL run-defining.
Fields: **Trigger** (when it can appear), **Scene** (presentation), **Outcomes** (choices and results), **Reward/Cost**, **Variations**.
Systems named (Bond, Nature, Memory, Chronicle, Mastery, Nemesis, Legacy, Rival) already exist in the game, so each event can be data.

**RE-A01 Midnight Snack Raid** · C · S
Trigger: rest stop, a party member with a hearty nature or low-ish HP. Scene: camp at night, a Pokémon sneaks to the food bag. Outcomes: scold it (Bond -1, keeps berries), share the snack (Bond +2, lose 1 berry), join in (whole party Bond +1, lose 3 berries). Reward/Cost: berries for Bond. Variations: Snorlax-line eats everything; Jolly nature drags a friend along.

**RE-A02 Who Gets the Last Bed** · C · S
Trigger: Pokémon Center or inn stop with 2+ injured members. Scene: two Pokémon stare at one bed. Outcomes: pick one (it heals fully, other heals half, loser sulks one battle), split it (both heal 3/4), flip a coin (random, no sulk). Reward/Cost: healing trade. Variations: rivals by type chart sulk longer.

**RE-A03 The Sulk** · C · S
Trigger: a Pokémon sat out 4+ battles in a row. Scene: it turns its back on the party. Outcomes: promise it the lead next battle (it gets +10% damage once), give a treat (Bond +1), ignore it (stays benched, Bond -2). Reward/Cost: bench fairness. Variations: Proud/Naughty natures refuse treats.

**RE-A04 Starter Remembers the First Day** · U · M
Trigger: starter at Bond high and run past stage 3. Scene: a flashback montage of the first route. Outcomes: reply gently (starter gains a one-time Memory "Where It Started"), sell the moment short (nothing), invite others to watch (all Bond +1). Reward/Cost: unique Memory. Variations: art differs per starter line.

**RE-A05 Unspoken Rivalry Inside the Team** · U · M
Trigger: two members of the same type or role, both with the top KO count. Scene: they race each other on the trail. Outcomes: judge it a draw (both +1 Bond), let it ride (winner gets +2 Speed for the stage, loser -1 mood), split their jobs (assign roles; each gets a small stat tilt). Reward/Cost: small stat tilts. Variations: lasts several stages if encouraged.

**RE-A06 Teach Me How** · C · S
Trigger: a veteran Pokémon (high level, high Mastery) beside a fresh recruit. Scene: the recruit copies its stance. Outcomes: allow tutoring (recruit gains XP equal to one battle), step in (nothing), pair them in battle (recruit gets a one-battle ability hint). Reward/Cost: free XP. Variations: tutor's nature changes the lesson text.

**RE-A07 Fear of the Dark Tunnel** · C · S
Trigger: cave or night node, a party member with a timid nature. Scene: it freezes at the tunnel mouth. Outcomes: carry it (Bond +1, you lose nothing), send a braver friend to lead (friend +1, scared one safe), push on (scared one starts next fight flinching). Reward/Cost: courage trade. Variations: Ghost-types find it hilarious.

**RE-A08 Jealous of the Newcomer** · U · M

**RE-A09 Shared Umbrella** · C · S
