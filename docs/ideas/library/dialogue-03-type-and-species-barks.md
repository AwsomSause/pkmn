# Dialogue 03: Type, species, and form barks (230 lines)

Context-gated lines for specific Pokémon types, species, and forms. `ideas/dialogue.md` has no type- or species-specific voice. Format: `- [trigger | gate] text`. `{A}` = the Pokémon. Triggers: `send` sent out, `crit`, `low` low HP, `ko`, `win`, `idle` hub/rest, `weather`, `evolve`.

## Normal
- [send] {A} cracks its neck and gives {player} a thumbs-up, in Pokémon.
- [low] {A} wheezes, but plants its feet. Ordinary doesn't mean easy.
- [ko] {A} is done for the day and proud of how little complaining it did.
- [win] {A} grins like it just won the lottery of boring victories.
- [idle] {A} watches a cloud for forty minutes. No complaints.
- [weather: rain] {A} stands in the puddle and calls it a day well spent.
- [evolve] {A} looks at its new self and says it knew it all along.
- [crit] {A} stares at its own paw. "Did I do that?"
