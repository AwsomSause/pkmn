## Prioritization framework

Rule of thumb: Essential = foundations plus the biggest lift per cost; High = major gameplay value; Medium = build when a slice has room; Low = polish; Experimental = needs a prototype and a go/no-go; Extremely Rare = tiny content meant to be discovered.
