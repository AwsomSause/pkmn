# Master Idea Library 03: Trainer Systems & Progression (TR-001 to TR-080)

Legend and fields as in file 01. Design only.

## A. Trainer identity and skills

**TR-001 Trainer Credo** — At run start pick a credo (Underdog, Collector, Duelist, Caretaker, Gambler) that rewrites one rule: Caretaker heals bond faster but gets fewer items; Gambler gets bigger odds both ways. *Trigger:* run start. *Feel:* a build identity beyond starter. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* trainer identity, starter sets. *Balance:* equal power across credos.

**TR-002 Field Skills Tree** — Trainer earns skill points from badges: Tracking (reveal nodes), Haggling (shop prices), First Aid (rest heal), Taming (catch rate). One tree, short, sharp. *Trigger:* badge. *Feel:* choices matter. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* mastery. *Balance:* avoid mandatory picks.

**TR-003 Trainer Titles** — Titles earned from play style (Iron Nurse, Berry Baron, The Retreater) show on the card and shift NPC greetings. *Trigger:* thresholds. *Feel:* identity from behavior. *Rarity:* Long-term. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* stats, dialogue. *Balance:* cosmetic.

**TR-004 Trainer Fatigue** — The trainer has Stamina; long streaks without rest reduce command accuracy ("Trainer shouts hoarse"). *Trigger:* battles in a row. *Feel:* human limits. *Rarity:* Common. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* rest. *Balance:* mild.

**TR-005 Specialisation Badges** — Beyond gym badges, earn specialist badges (win with only Normal types, no items, solo) shown on a rack. *Trigger:* feats. *Feel:* creative challenges. *Rarity:* Long-term. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* achievements. *Balance:* rewards cosmetic plus small perks.

**TR-006 Trainer Backstory Roll** — A procedural backstory (ex-ranger, orphaned breeder) adds one starting trait and gets referenced in dialogue. *Trigger:* run start. *Feel:* every run a different protagonist. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* dialogue, quests. *Balance:* traits small.

**TR-007 Trainer Bag Tetris** — Limited grid-based bag where item shapes matter (Rare Candy tiny, Repel long). *Trigger:* inventory. *Feel:* resource tension. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* items, UI. *Balance:* QoL option.

**TR-008 Reputation Axes** — Reputation along Kind/Ruthless and Flashy/Humble gates NPC reactions and event options. *Trigger:* choices. *Feel:* consequences. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* events. *Balance:* hidden but readable.

**TR-009 Mentor Selection** — Pick a mentor at the start (retired champion, weird scientist, grandma) who sends occasional letters with tips and unique bonuses. *Trigger:* run start. *Feel:* narrative scaffolding. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* letters. *Balance:* equal.

**TR-010 Trainer Fashion** — Outfits earned in runs give tiny perks (rain jacket reduces weather penalties). *Trigger:* rewards. *Feel:* identity. *Rarity:* Uncommon. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* sprites. *Balance:* tiny.

**TR-018 Coach Notes** — Pokémon summary shows your own typed note ("never put in rain"); notes appear in later runs. *Trigger:* player. *Feel:* personal. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* S. *Deps:* save. *Balance:* none.

**TR-019 Team Roles** — Assign roles (Lead, Anchor, Closer) with role-specific mini passives. *Trigger:* party menu. *Feel:* structure. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* M. *Deps:* party. *Balance:* flat.

**TR-020 Battlefield Tactics Cards** — Draft tactic cards between acts (Sandbag, Blitz) that can be played once. *Trigger:* act break. *Feel:* deckbuilding tinge. *Rarity:* Always. *Cx:* L. *Deps:* draft modifiers. *Balance:* catalogue.
